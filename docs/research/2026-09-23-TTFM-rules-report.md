# TTrades Fractal Model (TTFM) — Implementable Rule Specification

Sources are TTrades' own free blog/PDF pages (primary) plus one indicator feature list. TTrades' actual Pine source is invite-only, so indicator-internal formulas below are marked as inference where the blog does not state them. Notation: `C1`=candle before swing, `C2`=swing candle, `C3`=after C2, `C4`=expansion candle. Timeframes: higher (HTF) closure + lower (LTF) CISD.

## 1. Overall structure & swing-point validity

- **Structure.** Bias on the highest TF (daily), swing/wick structure one TF down (4H or 1H), entry/confirmation 2 TFs down (15M or 5M). Canonical pairings stated: Daily→Hourly→5M; Daily→4H→15M; Hourly→15M→1M (scalping); Hourly→5M→1M (with 1M inversion). Monthly pairs with daily, intraday pairs with lower intraday — the model is fully fractal and the rules do not change with TF ([playbook](https://ttrades.com/fractal-model-playbook-aligning-daily-hourly-and-5-minute-charts/), [best-TF](https://ttrades.com/the-best-timeframes-for-ttrades-fractal-model-simple/), [CISD](https://ttrades.com/how-change-in-the-state-of-delivery-cisd-confirms-swing-points/)).
- **The validity rule (mechanical, two ANDed conditions).** "A swing is only valid when it has both: • A point of interest • A valid closure (candle 2 or candle 3). If either is missing, the swing point is ignored." ([Candle 3 guide](https://ttrades.com/candle-3-closure-a-complete-guide-to-identifying-continuations-and-reversals/)). Extended three-part form: HTF closure **and** LTF CISD inside that HTF candle, at a POI. "If CISD is missing, I ignore the setup completely" ([CISD](https://ttrades.com/how-change-in-the-state-of-delivery-cisd-confirms-swing-points/)).
- **POI (point of interest) set.** FVG/imbalance, prior swing high/low, prior candle high/low, equal highs/lows, liquidity sweep, premium/discount array, HTF POI. If no FVG or swing exists, an **opposing candle** can serve as the POI ([C2 closures](https://ttrades.com/understanding-candle-2-closures-within-the-fractal-model/), [Ideal Formation](https://ttrades.com/ttrades-ideal-formation-high-probability-swing-points/)).
- **Confidence:** high (explicit text). One genuine ambiguity: TTrades never defines a minimum FVG size or a POI-strength metric; implementation must impose one.

## 2. Candle 1/2/3 closures

- **Candle numbering:** C1 = before the swing point; C2 = the swing high or low; C3 = after C2; C4 = after C3. Bearish→bullish: C2 is a swing low. Bullish→bearish: C2 is a swing high ([playbook](https://ttrades.com/fractal-model-playbook-aligning-daily-hourly-and-5-minute-charts/)).
- **Continuation closure:** price takes out the previous candle's high or low **and closes beyond it** → trend continues ([Candle 3 guide](https://ttrades.com/candle-3-closure-a-complete-guide-to-identifying-continuations-and-reversals/)).
- **Reversal closure:** price **sweeps** the previous candle's high or low (wick trades beyond it) but **closes back inside** the previous candle's range → reversal evidence. "A Candle 2 closure is always a reversal closure."
- **C2 closure, bullish:** sweep previous candle's **low**, close back **above** that low. **Bearish:** sweep previous candle's **high**, close back **below** that high ([C2 closures](https://ttrades.com/understanding-candle-2-closures-within-the-fractal-model/)). Valid C2 also requires a POI, else ignored. Anticipates C3 expansion in the reversal direction; if C3 closes beyond C2's high/low, a C4 expansion follows.
- **C3 closure (when C2 fails):** price does **not** sweep the previous candle, yet C3 **closes over the body of C2 and engulfs it** → bullish = close over C2 body; bearish = close below C2 body. It "closes beyond the opening price and range of candle 2" ([Candle 3 guide](https://ttrades.com/candle-3-closure-a-complete-guide-to-identifying-continuations-and-reversals/), [CISD](https://ttrades.com/how-change-in-the-state-of-delivery-cisd-confirms-swing-points/)). Anticipates C4 as expansion.
- **Equilibrium (50%) usage.** (a) For a C3 closure: mark C3's **full range**; bullish → expect C4 to **wick into the upper half of C3**; bearish → **wick into lower half** ([Candle 3 guide](https://ttrades.com/candle-3-closure-a-complete-guide-to-identifying-continuations-and-reversals/)). (b) For a large-wick C2: monitor **the midpoint of C2's wick**; C3 should respect it, else the swing is unlikely to hold. (c) The two levels to mark after an Ideal Formation: **the opening price of the opposing candle series** that created the swing, and **the EQ (50%) of the confirmation candle** ([Ideal Formation](https://ttrades.com/ttrades-ideal-formation-high-probability-swing-points/)).

## 3. CISD (Change In State Of Delivery)

- **Definition (three conditions, all required):** price reaches a **point of interest**; price **takes out liquidity forming a new high or low**; price **closes back through the candles that made that high or low** ([IC-CISD](https://ttrades.com/intracandle-cisd-ic-cisd-improve-your-entries/)). It is described as "the order block formation that occurs when trend shifts" ([CISD](https://ttrades.com/how-change-in-the-state-of-delivery-cisd-confirms-swing-points/)).
- **The series and the CISD level.** Bearish CISD: price forms a **higher high** and closes **below the candles that created the high**. Bullish CISD: price forms a **lower low** and closes **above the candles that created the low** (*ibid.*). The blog says "closes through the series of candles" and, for the Ideal Formation, "closes through the **opposing down-close/up-close candle series**." The exact price used as the drawn line is stated in one place only: **"the opening price of the opposing candle series that created the protected swing"** ([Ideal Formation](https://ttrades.com/ttrades-ideal-formation-high-probability-swing-points/)). **Inference / implementation note:** the level is the **open of the first candle of the consecutive same-direction (opposing) series** — e.g. for a bullish CISD, the open of the first down-close candle of the run into the low; a close above it is the CISD. TTrades also uses "opening price … and range of candle 2" for C3, i.e. the body (open) is the operative reference, not the wick extreme. Confidence: **high on the trigger logic, medium on the exact line** (the phrase varies between "series", "opening price of the series" and "body").
- **"Intra-candle" / early CISD (IC-CISD).** Applies the same CISD **inside a single HTF candle** to prove the wick is complete: HTF candle opens, trades against the intended direction forming a wick, LTF structure trends that opposing way, then an **LTF CISD against that short-term move** marks the wick as done → trade the body. Early-in-the-candle CISD is explicitly strongest; late CISD is to be skipped ([IC-CISD](https://ttrades.com/intracandle-cisd-ic-cisd-improve-your-entries/), [wick/body](https://ttrades.com/let-the-wick-form-trade-the-body-stop-getting-stopped-out/)). The indicator's "Early Trend Shift" toggle "automatically plots CISD before candle closure" ([indicator page](https://ttrades.com/fractal-model-indicator/)) — i.e. a **live/provisional** CISD evaluated on the unclosed candle, confirmed at close.
- **CISD → protected swing.** "When IC-CISD forms, a swing high or low becomes protected." A protected swing is created when **price closes through the series of opposing candles that led into a high or low** (bullish: sweep below a low → C2/C3 closes bullish → closure breaks the down-close series → protected swing low). Once formed, it is the **stop/invalidation** level and the level price should hold ([Ideal Formation](https://ttrades.com/ttrades-ideal-formation-high-probability-swing-points/), [IC-CISD](https://ttrades.com/intracandle-cisd-ic-cisd-improve-your-entries/)). **Ideal Formation** = a C2-or-C3 closure that **simultaneously** creates a protected swing.
- **Quality filter:** clean V-shaped reversal or strong displacement, decisive close through structure, minimal sideways consolidation. Choppy candle that "barely closes through structure" = consolidation, not CISD.

## 4. SMT (Smart Money Technique / Trap) divergence

- **Definition:** divergence between two correlated markets. When one makes a new high/low and the correlated one fails, SMT exists.
- **Bullish SMT:** Asset A makes a **lower low**, Asset B makes a **higher low** → relative strength, bullish reversal. **Bearish SMT:** Asset A makes a **higher high**, Asset B makes a **lower high** → relative weakness, bearish reversal ([Mastering SMT](https://ttrades.com/mastering-smt-spot-divergences-in-trading/)).
- **Which swings are compared:** the swings at the **C2 swing point**, classified by model position — **Reversal SMT** = between **C1 and C2** (usually "primary market makes a higher high, correlated makes a lower high" at the swept level); **Continuation SMT** = between **C2 and C3** (one market takes out a prior high/low, the other fails); **Double SMT** = both present ([How To Use SMT](https://ttrades.com/how-to-use-smt-divergence-ttrades-fractal-model/)). SMT also appears *inside* an FVG when one asset retraces into the gap and the other does not ([Mastering SMT](https://ttrades.com/mastering-smt-spot-divergences-in-trading/)).
- **Pairs.** NQ/ES (primary), correlated FX pairs (EURUSD & GBPUSD; EURGBP as the relative-strength proxy for EU vs GU), EURUSD vs **DXY** (inversely correlated), correlated precious metals ([Mastering SMT](https://ttrades.com/mastering-smt-spot-divergences-in-trading/), [EURGBP](https://ttrades.com/eurgbp-for-relative-strength-eu-vs-gu/)). **Implementation note:** the indicator has a "Reversal SMT" toggle that plots only reversal SMTs and only when aligned with Auto Bias ([indicator page](https://ttrades.com/fractal-model-indicator/)).
- **Confidence:** high. Ambiguity: the bar-index alignment for "corresponding" swings across two symbols (TTrades compares structure, not a fixed lookback) is not specified; a lookback/ATR-tolerance parameter must be chosen.

## 5. PSP (Precision Swing Point)

- **Definition:** a **difference in candle closures** between correlated assets — not a price divergence. When two correlated markets form the same overall structure but **one candle closes bullish while the other closes bearish**, that is a PSP ([Using PSP](https://ttrades.com/using-psp-to-confirm-swing-points-ttfm/)).
- **Candle 1 PSP:** C1 closes a different colour than the corresponding candle on the correlated asset. **Candle 2 PSP:** the difference appears on C2 instead. Both serve the same role.
- **Rules:** only meaningful inside an **already valid fractal model** and at a C2/C3 closure; it is confirmation, never a setup. Strongest usage: PSP + then wait for an **intra-candle CISD creating a new protected swing**, which becomes the stop. Complements SMT (SMT = price divergence, PSP = closure divergence). Fractal across timeframes.
- **Confidence:** high (explicit and unambiguous). The only parameter is the correlated symbol.

## 6. T-Spot

- **What it actually is:** a **price zone** — "the zone where I expect the wick of the higher timeframe candle to form." The indicator "auto plot[s] T-Spot support and resistance zones" ([1-min inversions](https://ttrades.com/refining-entries-with-the-fractal-model-precision-with-1-minute-inversions/), [indicator page](https://ttrades.com/fractal-model-indicator/)).
- **When it appears:** **structurally**, after the LTF/MTF CISD confirms — "Once confirmed, the indicator will plot the T-Spot, the expected wick zone." It is not triggered by a clock time in any documented source. Entry trigger is then a **1-minute inversion inside the T-Spot** (long: close above a bearish FVG; short: close below a bullish FVG), stop on the invalidation point, target 2R or a time exit (e.g. next hourly open). If the T-Spot forms without a wick, anticipate a retracement into it before expansion; avoid inversions late in the HTF candle.
- **Session anchoring:** the related TTrades lesson is literally titled *Expansions / Understanding T-Spot* (PDF, no fetchable text) and the London lesson says the kill zone "is not extremely important… the setup matters more than the specific time", with 4H-profile reversals often around the **2200 or 02:00** candle ([London](https://ttrades.com/how-to-trade-london-using-ttrades-fractal-model/), [T-spot PDF](https://ttrades.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Understanding20T-spot20TTrades20PDF.pdf)). **Confidence: high** that T-Spot is the HTF-candle wick zone derived from structure, **low** that it is anchored to fixed clock times (midnight open / 08:30 / 09:30 is **not** documented in any source I could read — treat as unverified). A reasonable MQL5 implementation: a band spanning the area of the current/last HTF candle where the wick is expected, i.e. anchored at the HTF open ± a range fraction, drawn as a zone until the protected swing confirms.

## 7. Auto Targets / Fibonacci projections

- **Anchor rule (mechanical).** Anchor a Fibonacci tool to the **manipulation leg** — the swing that **sweeps liquidity and occurs immediately before the CISD**: the **final high before a bearish CISD** or the **final low before a bullish CISD**. Bearish setups are projected **high→low**; bullish setups **low→high**. Start the construction **from Candle 2, the reversal point** ([Std-dev projections](https://ttrades.com/standard-deviation-projections/), [Projections guide](https://ttrades.com/understanding-projections-in-the-fractal-model-a-complete-guide/)).
- **Levels.** Negative Fibonacci extensions from the leg: the std-dev article lists **1, 0, −1, −2, −2.5, −4**. The indicator markets "Fibonacci Targets" and "Auto Targets… liquidity levels" ([indicator page](https://ttrades.com/fractal-model-indicator/)).
- **Expectation by leg size.** Average leg → **−2 to −2.5** (primary reaction/low-hanging fruit); expanding leg → **−4 to −4.5** (max expansion); **large** manipulation leg → reduce expectation to **−1**. Displacement closing beyond −2/−2.5 raises continuation probability toward max expansion. Multiple overlapping projections (one leg's −4 aligning with another's −2/−2.5) = high-probability zone.
- **Liquidity targets (separate, and primary).** Bullish → previous **untouched** highs; bearish → previous **untouched** lows; prior HTF candle highs/lows, HTF dealing-range extreme, failure swings, equal highs/lows. A level already taken out is invalid. Progression: 5M entry → hourly swing; hourly setup → daily swing; daily setup → weekly swing. Minimum 2R; runners to the HTF draw on liquidity ([Targets](https://ttrades.com/how-to-set-price-targets-using-the-fractal-model/), [playbook](https://ttrades.com/fractal-model-playbook-aligning-daily-hourly-and-5-minute-charts/)).
- **Confidence:** high on anchoring and on the −1/−2/−2.5/−4 mapping; medium on whether TTrades' indicator uses exactly `1,0,−1,−2,−2.5,−4` (the second article truncated its level list).

## 8. Auto Bias / HTF trend filter

- **Indicator mechanic (documented).** Bias filter has five options: Neutral (both directions), Bullish, Bearish, **Auto Bias 1** = "automatically references a higher timeframe fractal model **one timeframe above** your selected fractal", **Auto Bias 2** = **two timeframes above**. Worked example given verbatim: with the hourly + 5-minute fractal model, Auto Bias 1 references the **4H and 15M** model, Auto Bias 2 references the **Daily and 1H** model. Setups are only plotted when aligned with that higher timeframe model ([full guide](https://ttrades.com/ttrades-fractal-model-indicator-full-guide/)).
- **So mechanically:** Auto Bias is **not** a moving average or a simple candle-vs-open rule — it is the **same fractal model (closure + CISD/protected swing) evaluated on the next-higher fractal alignment**, and its direction gates the lower-TF plots.
- **Bias determination (the underlying trend read), documented forms:** (a) close **below the previous day's low** → expect continuation lower; **failure to close below** the previous day's low → expect reversal higher (and the mirror for the previous day's high); (b) a C2 or C3 **reversal closure at a POI**; (c) **EQ of the previous day's range** separates reversal days (framed off the prior high/low) from continuation days (framed off EQ, and "lower-timeframe closures do not occur beyond that level") ([playbook](https://ttrades.com/fractal-model-playbook-aligning-daily-hourly-and-5-minute-charts/), [Easy Daily Bias](https://ttrades.com/easy-daily-bias-a-mechanical-trading-framework/)).
- **Confidence:** high on (a)-(c) and on Auto Bias 1/2 semantics; medium on the precise internal formula the closed-source indicator uses.

## 9. Other plotted elements

From the indicator page and full guide ([indicator](https://ttrades.com/fractal-model-indicator/), [full guide](https://ttrades.com/ttrades-fractal-model-indicator-full-guide/)):

- **HTF Candles overlay** — adjustable count (default **4**), Size, Offset (17 recommended for a second stacked instance), Body/Border/Wick colours, HTF time labels.
- **HTF Open** — plots the open of the corresponding higher-TF fractal candle (e.g. the hourly open on a 5M chart).
- **Vertical Lines** — mark each new higher-TF period using the same fractal alignment.
- **L/H Lines** — prior HTF candle **low/high** (liquidity levels); off by default.
- **Previous EQ** — 50% of the previous HTF candle's range ("a vital component").
- **Drawing Type** — On Chart / HTF candle / Both.
- **CISD + Early CISD (Trend Shift)** — trend-shift line and its pre-close provisional version.
- **T-Spot zones**, **SMT (reversal SMTs)**, **Auto Targets (liquidity)**, **Fibonacci Targets (projections)**, **Auto Bias**.
- **Dealing ranges** (manual in the blog, implied by "Previous EQ"/L/H lines in the indicator): range = from the swing you are trading away from **to the HTF swing that created it** (bullish: C2 low → the HTF swing high that made that low). Split at **50%**: upper half **premium**, lower half **discount**, **EQ** = middle. Longs in **discount or at EQ**, shorts in **premium or at EQ**. Target = the opposite extreme (dealing-range high for a bullish range). Ranges are fractal and may overlap across timeframes ([Dealing Ranges](https://ttrades.com/understanding-dealing-ranges-in-trading-get-better-risk-reward/)).
- **History** — number of past fractal models retained (0 = current only; each step adds one bullish and one bearish). **C2/C3/C4** toggles hide/show each closure type. **Fractal** = Auto / fixed / Custom (Custom Fractal drop-downs). **Alerts** are real-time against current settings.
- **FVG/inversion FVG (iFVG)** are used as POIs and 1M entry triggers; inversion = close through the gap in the opposite direction ([1-min inversions](https://ttrades.com/refining-entries-with-the-fractal-model-precision-with-1-minute-inversions/)).
